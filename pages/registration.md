## Fees

| | Early<br>Dec&nbsp;1&nbsp;– Feb&nbsp;19 | Regular<br>Feb&nbsp;20&nbsp;– Apr&nbsp;1 |
|---|---|---|
| Student | $30 | $50 |
| Non-student | $125 | $150 |
| Friday dinner | $40 | $45 |

Registration includes refreshments and the poster reception. The Friday dinner is
a separate purchase, and seats are limited.
